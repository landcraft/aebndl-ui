# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Home server or NAS admins running the service via a Docker container. They frequently monitor and queue downloads from various devices, including mobile phones.

## Product Purpose
A clean, resilient web dashboard for queueing, monitoring, and managing AEBN video downloads running remotely.

## Brand Personality
Minimalist, modern, and highly intuitive utility.

## Anti-references
Cluttered and over-engineered interfaces, complex enterprise tables, and generic outdated template designs.

## Design Principles
1. **Mobile-Responsive Core:** Tapping, viewing status, and adding URLs must be effortless on small screens.
2. **High Signal-to-Noise:** Focus entirely on job status and progress without decorative clutter.
3. **Immediate Clarity:** The user should know exactly what's downloading, what failed, and what to do next at a glance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Ensure proper color contrast (especially in dark mode), adequately sized tap targets for mobile touch interactions, and clear, descriptive feedback states.
